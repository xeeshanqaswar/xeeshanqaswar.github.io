1. Summary

  • You can play without creating an account. We sign you in as a guest using an identifier generated from your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device’s advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads.
  • Your in-game progress (player name, coins, unlocked items, outfits) is stored on your device.
  • The Game does not access your camera, photos, contacts, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information we collect

Type Examples Source & purpose
Device identifiers A device-generated identifier used for your guest account; the Android advertising ID Collected automatically. Used to sign you in as a guest, and by our advertising partner to show and measure ads.
Device & technical data Device model, operating system version, language, app version, IP address, network type Collected automatically by the Game and our service providers to run the Game, deliver ads and prevent fraud. An approximate location (country/region) may be derived from your IP address.
Usage & ad interaction data Ads viewed or tapped, rewarded videos completed Collected by Google AdMob to deliver rewards, measure ad performance and prevent invalid traffic.
Game data on your device Player name you enter, coins, unlocked items, outfits, settings Stored locally on your device so you can keep playing. We do not upload it to our servers.

We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, payment details, photos or contacts.

3. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the Game, including your guest sign-in and saved progress.
  • To show ads, give you in-game rewards for watching rewarded videos, and measure ad performance.
  • To keep the Game secure and to detect and prevent fraud, abuse and invalid ad traffic.
  • To understand and fix technical problems and improve the Game.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Where the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we rely on your consent for personalised advertising, and on our legitimate interests (running, securing and improving the Game) or contract (providing the Game you chose to play) for the other purposes.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows banner-free, full-screen ads: interstitial ads (for example when you return to the home screen after finishing a competition) and rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for coins or items.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). Google and its partners may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to serve ads, including personalised ads where you have agreed or where the law allows, and to measure and improve them. 5. Third-party services

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Game, under their own privacy terms:

  • Google AdMob – advertising. Privacy policy
  • Microsoft Azure PlayFab – guest sign-in and game backend, using your device-generated identifier. Privacy statement
  • Unity Technologies – the game engine used to build the Game. Privacy policy

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users’ safety, or as part of a merger or sale of our business.

6. Consent & your choices

  • Consent for ads (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before ads are personalised, the Game shows a consent message from Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can accept or decline. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).
  • Game data: uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage in your device settings, deletes the progress stored on your device.
  • Stop all collection: you can stop all collection of information by uninstalling the Game.

7. Retention & security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Guest account identifiers held by our backend provider are kept while your account is in use and deleted on request. Our service providers keep data according to their own retention policies.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, or the CCPA/CPRA and other U.S. state laws), you may have the right to:

  • access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time;
  • receive a copy of your data (portability);
  • opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising — use the device advertising settings above or contact us;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Because the Game has no login with your name or email, we may need your guest User ID (shown on the Game’s loading screen) to locate your data.

9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the Game or on its store page.
