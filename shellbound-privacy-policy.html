Shellbound Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Arcanine Studios

In short
  • Shellbound has no accounts and we never ask for your name, email or location.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

Who we are

Shellbound (Android app com.arcaninestudios.shellbound) is developed and published by Arcanine Studios ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, contact us at xeeshanqaswar@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • levels and chapters completed, best times, lives and collected hearts;
  • music, sound and mute settings.

This information is not sent to us. We do not run servers for the game and do not use analytics of our own. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

Advertising by Google AdMob

Shellbound is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: short full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for a hint, extra time, a retry or to skip a level. To serve and measure these ads, Google may collect or receive:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • device and app information such as device model, Android version, language and app version;
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information used to keep ads working and prevent fraud.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, show personalized ads. Google acts under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Information sent to Google travels over encrypted connections.

Your consent and privacy choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can accept, manage your options or decline. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalized ads, which use limited data.

To change your choice later, open the pause menu and tap Privacy. Depending on the laws where you live, for example in some US states, this button may also let you opt out of personalized advertising.

On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings under Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).

Children

Shellbound is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information about you on any server, most requests are handled through your device settings and the in-game Privacy button described above. For information held by Google, use My Ad Center or Google's privacy tools. You may also contact us, or complain to your local data protection authority.

Data retention

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Google keeps the information it receives according to its own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or the law changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
