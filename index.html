How I work

Hands-on engineering with technical ownership.

I am most effective in roles where I can stay close to the code while owning larger technical outcomes. I have led developers, reviewed code, mentored junior engineers, participated in hiring, broken requirements into implementation plans, made architecture decisions, managed releases, and worked directly with international clients, gameplay teams, producers, and research scientists.

I am comfortable joining an existing codebase, understanding its constraints, and deciding whether a system should be extended, isolated, optimized, or rewritten. The goal is not architecture for its own sake; it is to make the product easier to ship, operate, and evolve.

AI tools speed up my research, boilerplate, and documentation; architecture, review, profiling, and final engineering decisions stay engineer-owned.

About

I build systems that have to survive production.

I’m a hands-on Senior / Lead Unity Engineer with experience owning production systems across games, multiplayer products, mobile applications, and scientific research software. My strongest work tends to begin where a project stops being straightforward: integrating a major capability into an existing codebase, diagnosing memory or performance failures, designing a feature that must evolve for years, or coordinating implementation across technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Over my career I have led developers and architected substantial Unity systems while remaining directly involved in implementation. Projects such as SPACE, G.I. Joe: Wrath of Cobra, and Pro Golf reflect different sides of that work — long-term product ownership, real-time multiplayer integration, and production architecture under memory/content constraints.

I’m continuing to deepen my systems, networking, and software-engineering foundations while building toward technical leadership, consulting, and eventually product ownership.
